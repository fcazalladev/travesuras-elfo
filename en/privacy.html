Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Privacy by Design (Local First)

The "Magic Elf Pranks" application has been designed with maximum respect for your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. All the content you generate in the app (such as your favorite pranks or your calendar planning) is saved exclusively locally on your own device.

2. Use of Permissions (Camera and Gallery)

The application may request access to your device's camera and photo gallery. This permission is used solely and exclusively so you can capture and save memories of your own pranks within the application. No images leave your device or are uploaded to any external server.

3. Third-Party Services

To keep the app free, we may use standard advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) that could collect basic device identifiers to serve non-personalized ads. We do not integrate invasive analytics tracking your personal identity.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact the developer through the channels provided in the Legal Notice.
